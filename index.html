About

A bit about my path

I currently teach Creative Writing and Graphic Narratives at the Institute of American Indian Arts in Santa Fe, where I build courses around native authors and artists and help students carry their stories into print. I also teach Composition and Creative Writing at Cuyamaca College, where I founded and mentor the Cuyamaca Creative, a student literary magazine, and helped bring its first print edition to life.

My own writing lives at the intersection of comics and memoir, rooted in the landscape I grew up in. I hold an MFA in Creative Writing from San Diego State University and a BA in Creative Writing from UC Santa Cruz. I'm fluent in English and Spanish, and certified through UMOJA and UndocuAdvocate to better support Black and undocumented students in the classroom.
